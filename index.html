A Practical, Focused Approach

An initial engagement can be structured as a Safety Program Diagnostic, giving management a clear picture of current conditions and a prioritized plan for improvement.

01 — REVIEW

Understand the Program

Review policies, procedures, incident history, investigations, and available safety performance information.

02 — IDENTIFY

Find the Causes

Interview key personnel and identify recurring hazards, contributing factors, and gaps between written procedures and actual practices.

03 — IMPROVE

Build the Action Plan

Develop prioritized corrective actions and practical processes for implementation, accountability, and follow-up.
